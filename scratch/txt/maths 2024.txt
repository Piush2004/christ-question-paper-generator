Course Outcomes (COs):    The students will able to  
CO1: Demonstrate a working knowledge of set notation and elementary set theory and 
            recognize the connection between set operations and logic. 
CO2: Apply the different properties of injections, surjections, bijections, compositions, and inverse 
functions. 
CO3: Understand different types of matrices and operations among matrices. 
CO4: Determine when a relation is reflexive, symmetric, antisymmetric, transitive, apply the 
           properties of equivalence relations and partial orderings, and explain the connection between 
           equivalence relations. 
CO5: Effective express the elementary concepts and results of Number theory.
